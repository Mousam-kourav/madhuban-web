## Executive findings

1. **The site lives on brand search.** The top 30 queries by clicks are dominated by branded terms: variants of "madhuban eco retreat", "madhuban resort bhopal", "madhuban resort by somaiya", plus typo variants ("madhubhan", "madhubani", "madhuvan"). These are 25 of the top 30 queries and account for ~85% of all clicks. **Any NEW build that weakens brand prominence in titles/H1 is immediately a CTR liability.** The duplicated-brand-suffix bug in NEW meta titles (Step 4 finding) particularly hurts here because the brand appears 2–3× and gets truncated in SERPs.

2. **Blog content is carrying ~80–100 clicks/quarter that NEW cannot serve.** Five OLD blog URLs in the top-20 traffic pages return 404 on NEW. Most traffic-bleeding 404: `/blogs/trekking-near-bhopal-15-best-treks-for-nature-adventure` — **52 clicks / 9,989 impressions / position 4.55**. This is the single highest-impression page after the homepage. Until it's either migrated or 301'd, ~10K monthly impressions evaporate at launch.

3. **`/day-outing` is the #2 traffic page and is preserved on NEW.** Good news — it gets 96 clicks / 5,525 impressions ranking #6.5 for "resorts near bhopal for day outing" and adjacent queries. NEW has this page, so no migration risk. But the NEW `/day-outing` page is a static React component with hardcoded content (see Step 4 extracts) — ensure it carries forward the keyword phrasing that earns those impressions.

4. **One unknown blog URL is bleeding 793 impressions:** `/blogs/madhuban-eco-retreat-complete-guide` (2 clicks, 793 impr, position 8.5). This post is NOT in the OLD `sitemap.xml`, so it was missed during Step 3 inventory — Google indexed it independently. It also 404s on NEW. Verify with the architect whether it's a forgotten draft post on OLD or genuine content worth restoring.

5. **`/hotels/madhuban-eco-retreat/` is a legacy URL with 1,605 impressions / 10 clicks** that is NOT covered by any redirect in `next.config.ts`. The slug suggests this is from an even older WordPress-era URL structure. **Add a 301** to `/` (or `/about-us`) before launch — currently it 404s on NEW.

6. **The "best hiking trails" cluster is sitting at high impressions but near-zero CTR.** Queries like "best hiking trails near me" (1,422 impr, 0.07% CTR), "best hiking trails nearby" (1,361 impr, 0% CTR), "hiking trails" (1,358 impr, 0% CTR) — these are extremely high-volume keyword stems where Madhuban appears in results but with snippets that don't match user intent. The page Google associates with these is the `trekking-near-bhopal-15-best-treks-for-nature-adventure` blog post. **If that blog is migrated and given more locale-specific snippet text, this is the single biggest impression → click conversion opportunity.**

7. **International traffic exists but is negligible.** India = 3,066 clicks (96%). Top non-India: US (14 clicks), UK (11), Canada (7), Netherlands (6), Germany (4). Don't deprioritize India targeting for international polish.
