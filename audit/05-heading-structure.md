## 1. Summary of structural violations

Definitions:
- **NO_H1** — page has zero `<h1>` elements. Fail.
- **MULTIPLE_H1** — page has more than one `<h1>`. Confuses crawlers about page topic.
- **SKIP_LEVEL** — heading hierarchy jumps a level (e.g. H2 directly to H4). Hurts accessibility and screen-reader navigation.
- **H1_NOT_FIRST** — H1 appears after another heading. Crawlers expect H1 first.
