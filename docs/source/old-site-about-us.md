## Section 1: About the Retreat (Overview)

Madhuban Eco Retreat is a regenerative forest and experiential travel destination spanning **20 acres of previously barren and degraded land**. The resort combines rustic charm with comfort and invites guests to reconnect with nature's rhythms.

**Strategic Location:**
Positioned near Bhopal and close to Ratapani and Satpura Tiger Reserves, serving as an ideal base for exploring wildlife, cultural heritage, and natural beauty of central India.

**Key Identity Points:**
- One of the best resorts near Ratapani
- A peaceful jungle resort near Bhopal
- A natural space for detox retreats and wellness
- A luxury eco-resort offering sustainable forest living

**Activities & Attractions Highlighted:**
- Wildlife safaris
- Bhimbetka UNESCO World Heritage Site exploration
- Buddhist monastery visits

## Section 2: The Madhuban Story

The venture originated from an emotional response to modern world disconnection from nature. The **Somaiya Group** — an 80-year-old corporate entity with expertise in education, healthcare, agriculture, social development, and sustainability — realized this vision. This represents their **first hospitality venture**, built on:
- Nature-based eco-friendly tourism
- Community empowerment
- Local livelihood generation
- Environmental responsibility

**Guest Demographics:**
Families, solo travelers, artists, researchers, nature lovers, and spiritual seekers

## Section 4: Vision & Mission

**Vision:**
"To become India's leading eco-luxury stay destination in Ratapani and a defining nature retreat near Bhopal, where guests rediscover the joy of simple living, reconnect with nature, and leave with a renewed sense of well-being."

Envisions travel supporting environment rather than harming it, where stays contribute to planetary healing.

**Mission:**
Offering deeply meaningful, earth-friendly travel experiences through:
- Sustainable, eco-conscious accommodation
- Organic, farm-to-table food from local produce
- Nature-based activities (forest walks, meditation, yoga, cultural immersion)
- Strong local community partnerships
- Experiences promoting mental clarity, wellness, and slow living

---

## Section 5: Core Values

1. **Sustainability:** Planet-first decision making from construction to cuisine
2. **Community:** Hand-in-hand collaboration with local artisans, farmers, and tribal communities
3. **Simplicity:** "The most profound experiences often come from the simplest moments"
4. **Authenticity:** Genuineness in food, architecture, and shared stories
5. **Learning:** Guest participation in organic farming, birdwatching, and cultural exchange
